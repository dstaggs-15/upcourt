# Playing Upocourt

## Your first game

Choose Exhibition, pick different home and away clubs, and start. Your team is the home selection. The court uses the same perspective and pixel drawing conventions as Downfield: move W toward the far hoop, S toward the near hoop, and A/D across the floor. Possession changes automatically switch control to your ball handler or a nearby defender.

You control both ends of the floor. Home attacks the far basket and away attacks the near basket throughout this arcade game. When you receive the ball, teammates follow the current play. When you lose possession, they recover to defensive assignments.

Shootaround is the easiest place to learn the meter. In Three Point, your shooter stays at the rack and advances after each shot; movement is disabled during the contest.

## Shooting

Hold Space/X to start the meter and release to stop it. The marker sweeps forward and then back. A close finish targets the midpoint; a jump shot or free throw targets the later green area. Waiting too long automatically releases a poor shot.

Shot quality combines distance, shooting or finishing skill, fatigue, defender proximity, active contests and release timing. The feedback line shows EARLY, GOOD, GREEN or LATE, then OPEN, CONTESTED or SMOTHERED, and the calculated make probability. A well-timed open shot within normal range gets a large bonus. A green release is not a guarantee through heavy pressure or from extreme distance.

Inside finishes use finishing skill and a shorter flight. Very close attempts by strong finishers use the dunk presentation. Long shots use shooting skill. Corner threes use the shorter corner distance; arc shots use 23.75 feet. A foot on the arc counts as two.

Shot Assist reduces the timing penalty for new matches. Rookie, Pro and All Star also change user shooting assistance and defensive steal pressure. They do not replace independent CPU rosters with copies of your team.

While charging, press F/Y to pump fake and cancel the attempt. Nearby defenders may leave their feet. Move into the space before trying again.

## Passing and creating space

E/A chooses a teammate using forward position and defensive pressure. Numbers 1–5 target PG, SG, SF, PF and C. Ctrl+E or LB+A sends a slower, higher lob. Passing lanes matter: a defender close to the flight can intercept.

F/Y performs a crossover when you have the ball. It spends a little energy and may slow a nearby defender; a failed move can be stripped. Use R/RB to bring the center toward a screen position. Stock screening plays also bring players through screen-and-roll waypoints. These are arcade spatial screens, not a full contact physics simulation.

P opens the playbook and pauses the court. Calling a play resets its waypoint clock. Your controlled player remains under your control; AI teammates run the authored movement. The 24 stock calls have distinct waypoint layouts.

## Defense and rebounding

Q/B chooses your player nearest the ball. Move into the ball handler's path. E/A reaches for a steal when close; an unsuccessful reach can become a foul. Space/X jumps to contest or block. A block requires proximity and timing during the early part of the shot flight.

R/RB holds a defensive contest/box-out pose. Reach the expected rebound spot before the ball lands; proximity, rebounding skill and an active action affect who wins the board. Offensive rebounds reset the shot clock to 14 seconds. Defensive rebounds start a fresh 24-second possession.

Pause → Defense cycles man-to-man, 2–3 zone and full-court pressure for your team. Man defense follows matchups, zone uses five floor anchors, and pressure follows opponents tightly across the court. Opposing defenders use their own matchups and lane reactions.

## Clocks, fouls and overtime

- Four regulation quarters. Options offers 60, 90, 120 or 180 seconds per quarter; 90 is the default.
- A possession has 24 seconds to release a shot. Pass flight consumes the shot clock.
- You have eight seconds to reach the frontcourt. Returning behind the backcourt threshold after crossing causes a turnover.
- Shooting fouls award two or three attempts; a made basket earns one additional attempt. A missed fouled attempt is not counted as a field-goal attempt.
- Five team fouls in a period trigger the non-shooting bonus. Team fouls reset each period.
- Six personal fouls disqualify a player. Substitutes fill vacancies when available.
- Three timeouts per half. A timeout requires your possession and cannot interrupt an airborne pass or shot. It restores some energy.
- The game stops clocks for inbounds, period breaks and free throws. A released shot can finish after the horn. Free throws from a shooting foul finish before the period changes.
- A tie after regulation starts a 30-second overtime. Further ties start another overtime. Finalized basketball fixtures never tie.

The game constrains players to the floor rather than running sideline inbound adjudication. It omits professional rules such as three-second violations, goaltending reviews and a full traveling/double-dribble model.

## Rotation and substitutions

Each roster player has a target number of minutes on a normalized 40-minute game scale. These are rotation targets, not guaranteed time. Short-quarter games scale the target accordingly.

Sprint spends energy more quickly. Low energy reduces movement and shooting. Bench players recover. Automatic substitutions consider fatigue, rotation targets, position eligibility and foul trouble at stoppages. Conditioning helps stamina; injured players cannot enter the match.

For a manual change, pause at a stoppage, choose Substitutions, choose a starter to remove, then an eligible healthy reserve. Live substitutions are rejected. You can disable automatic substitutions in Options or during a suspended match.

## Custom play designer

Open Playbook → Create a Play. Choose a position on the right, then click the floor to append waypoints for that player. Each route supports 12 points. Clear Route starts that player's path over. Name Play changes the title.

Save to Book persists the play; Use Play calls the current draft in a live game. Up to 40 custom plays can be stored alongside the 24 stock calls. The separate Edit entries reopen saved calls. Delete removes the selected saved call. Leaving the designer without saving does not add a play.

## Season and playoffs

Every club faces the other eleven at home and away: 22 rounds, six league fixtures each round. Completing or simulating your fixture settles every remaining game in the round and advances the league. If you have a postseason bye or have been eliminated, use Schedule → Sim Next / Advance to follow the remaining games.

Standings sort by wins, point differential, points scored, then permanent club ID. The top six qualify. Seeds one and two receive byes; three plays six and four plays five. The top seed draws the lower surviving seed in the semifinal. First round and semifinals are best of three; the final is best of five. Higher seeds host the first game and alternate venues thereafter.

Championships and each club's regular-season record are archived with the club's name at that time. Renaming or relocation does not rewrite history. MVP uses season production value: points + 1.2×rebounds + 1.5×assists + 3×steals + 3×blocks − 1.5×turnovers − 0.5×missed field goals. The leader screen uses that same production ordering. Defender emphasizes steals, blocks and boards. Rookie uses players with zero prior seasons of experience.

## Roster and contracts

The salary cap is $140M; the roster maximum is 15. Clubs retain at least eight players. Choose a roster player to inspect all eight basketball skills, age, potential, contract and injury status.

Starting positions require a matching primary or secondary role. When moving a current starter to a different position, the game needs an eligible replacement for the vacated spot. New signings enter the bench; choose their starting position yourself.

Free-agent salary is separate from its cash signing fee. The fee is $4M plus half the OVR above 70, rounded down. Signings receive three years. Renewals extend to four years, update salary to the rating-based demand and charge a renewal fee. You cannot renew a contract already at four years. Releasing a reserve costs a buyout of at least $1M or one-third of annual salary. Replace a starter before releasing him.

Trades begin with your outgoing player, then a club and incoming player. The review is available before execution. Both caps are checked. Player value includes rating, potential, age, remaining years, salary burden and injury recovery. CPU rebuilding clubs prefer youth, contenders protect current starters, and clubs value cover for the position they would lose. Accepted deals cost $3M and rebuild both lineups. The trade deadline starts at round 17.

## Development, staff and health

Training costs $3M once per round. Shooting, defense and conditioning focus different attributes. XP carries over; young players require less to develop, and improvements stop at potential. Playing or simulating games also grants development XP. Morale responds to training and results.

All six staff/facility tracks have five levels. An upgrade costs $8M plus $6M per existing level.

| Track | Effect |
|---|---|
| Offensive coach | Shooting and passing bonuses in new matches |
| Defensive coach | Defensive skill bonus in new matches |
| Conditioning coach | Match stamina and training XP |
| Training facility | More XP from training |
| Medical facility | Lower postgame injury probability |
| Arena | More home revenue |

Injuries are postgame league events and last a number of rounds. They can affect CPU clubs too. An injured starter is replaced from real roster players when the next match is created. Players age between seasons; older players lose speed and stamina and may retire.

## Cash, fans and the coach

Business displays cash, cap space, fans and owner confidence. Home revenue depends on fans, ticket price and arena level. Away games bring a smaller fixed amount. Every result charges operating costs, a share of payroll and staff costs. Higher tickets can reduce attendance. Wins tend to improve fans and confidence; losses reduce them. Championship winners receive a user-club cash bonus.

Change your mascot and color from Business. Moving to an unused city costs $15M and keeps player identities, schedule results and coach record. Another club cannot occupy the same city.

Coach wins, losses and titles follow you to another job. Change clubs before the first played game or between seasons. Very low confidence can cause dismissal after the season; choose a new job to continue the career.

## Offseason and draft

After the final, Transfers → Start Next Season Draft processes aging, retirement and contract expiration. Expired players join the market. CPU and user rosters receive minimum-roster replacement players where needed; these are real player records with salaries and IDs.

There are 36 prospects and 24 picks: two rounds for twelve clubs. Order is reverse regular-season record, with the champion moved to the final slot. CPU selections prefer rating and potential. Rookie contracts are three seasons at $3M per season. A pick needs one roster slot and $3M cap room; Pass Pick is available if you cannot accommodate a rookie. Prepare roster space before beginning the draft.

Once both rounds finish, undrafted prospects enter free agency, season production resets, all clubs rebuild lineups and the next 22-game schedule begins. Career totals and history remain.

## Saving and recovery

The local save contains career, settings, custom plays, contest best and the suspended match. It records actor positions, possession, clocks, shot flight, random sequence, fatigue, fouls, box scores and the active play. The save does not depend on an account or server.

The game writes a temporary XML file, flushes it, then atomically replaces the main file while keeping a backup. Invalid main saves trigger backup recovery with a visible notice. Keep a copy of the entire `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Upocourt` folder before manually editing saves.

Save and Home preserves the current game rather than forfeiting it. Finish it, or use Pause → Sim Rest, before changing the franchise roster, finances or schedule. Completed results are accepted only for the current unplayed fixture. Reopening a final screen cannot award cash or statistics twice.
